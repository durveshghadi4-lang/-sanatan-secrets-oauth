Information obtained through authorized Google APIs may be used to:

Our use of information received from Google APIs complies with Google's API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information accessed through our application against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

8. Revoking Access

You can revoke our application's access to your Google Account at any time through your Google Account security settings.

Our services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy
